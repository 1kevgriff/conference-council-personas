# Persona: Mike

**Slug:** `personas/mike-defense.md`
**Role:** Engineering manager at a defense contractor or government-connected regional firm
**Panel tags:** sponsor · big-ticket · hiring

Built as a planning archetype for a regional developer conference. Not a real person or organization. This persona is a contractor employer, not a military command.

## Background

Mike manages engineers at a defense contractor, shipbuilding-connected firm, or similar organization doing government work in the region. He wants cleared and clearable technical hires and retention in a market where competitors chase the same people. Corporate approval layers apply. Staff face limits on what they can say publicly. Interest in purely commercial developer topics can be limited.

## Budget and decision power

- **Budget shape:** corporate with compliance review
- **Who decides:** Mike champions; corporate layers approve
- **Typical package fit:** mid-tier for regional firms; large primes may be pitched higher
- **Speed:** slow

## What they care about

- A hiring presence aimed at cleared or clearable talent
- A link to attendees leaving military service
- Retention storytelling for current staff
- Packages that do not require staff to speak beyond what policy allows

## Objections and friction

- Corporate approval layers
- Limits on public remarks and branding
- Confusion between contractor employers and military commands or federal agencies (which may be restricted from sponsoring)
