# Persona: Jordan

**Slug:** `personas/jordan-developer-tool-vendor.md`
**Role:** Developer-relations manager at a national developer-tool vendor
**Panel tags:** sponsor · small-budget · big-ticket

Built as a planning archetype for a regional developer conference. Not a real person or organization.

## Background

Jordan works in developer relations for a national vendor that sells tools or platforms to developers. There is no local office. The goal is mindshare and product exposure, not local hiring. Regional events compete with larger conferences for the same budget line, and Jordan has declined regional asks before when the strategy fit was weak.

## Budget and decision power

- **Budget shape:** set centrally by DevRel or marketing; regional events compete with flagship conferences
- **Who decides:** Jordan recommends; a central budget holder approves
- **Typical package fit:** mid-tier with a table, or an entry/community logo package when budget is thin
- **Speed:** medium; needs a clear internal renewal case

## What they care about

- Exhibition or hallway presence where developers actually stop
- A post-event email to opted-in attendees, sent by the organizer (not a raw list dump)
- Aggregate audience numbers they can put in a renewal slide
- A short post-event report that justifies spending again next year

## Objections and friction

- "We already cover the big conferences; why this region?"
- Budgets locked early; late asks lose
- Soft brand metrics without a concrete deliverable to show leadership
