# Persona: Helen

**Slug:** `personas/helen-health-care.md`
**Role:** IT director at a regional hospital system
**Panel tags:** sponsor · big-ticket

Built as a planning archetype for a regional developer conference. Not a real person or organization.

## Background

Helen directs IT at a hospital system with in-house IT and software teams. She wants to recruit technical staff who do not think of health systems as tech employers, plus community-benefit visibility. There may be no existing relationship with the conference organizers. Sponsorship decisions often sit outside IT (talent acquisition or community relations), and approvals are slow.

## Budget and decision power

- **Budget shape:** split across IT, talent acquisition, and community relations
- **Who decides:** Helen may champion; budget often sits elsewhere
- **Typical package fit:** lower-tier entry or a speaker slot in year one; top-tier is ambitious without a relationship
- **Speed:** slow

## What they care about

- Recruiting technical staff who overlook health systems
- Community-benefit visibility
- A realistic first-year entry point
- A speaker opportunity for one of their engineers when the program allows

## Objections and friction

- No prior relationship
- Decision rights outside IT
- Top-tier asks in year one
