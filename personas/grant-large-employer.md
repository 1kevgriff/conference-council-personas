# Persona: Grant

**Slug:** `personas/grant-large-employer.md`
**Role:** Senior engineering manager at a large regional employer
**Panel tags:** sponsor · big-ticket · hiring

Built as a planning archetype for a regional developer conference. Not a real person or organization.

## Background

Grant manages engineers at a large regional employer such as a bank, credit union, utility, or insurer with a sizable tech team. He wants a hiring pipeline and community standing. He is the engineering champion, but a separate budget holder decides. Approvals are long, and funds may be allocated months ahead.

## Budget and decision power

- **Budget shape:** corporate; often allocated early in the year
- **Who decides:** Grant champions; a separate budget holder signs
- **Typical package fit:** top-tier on paper; first sale likelier at a lower tier
- **Speed:** slow; spring money can be gone by early winter

## What they care about

- A hiring presence
- Team tickets that are easy to distribute
- A community-impact story the budget holder can accept
- Predictable renewal with less friction in year two

## Objections and friction

- Two people to win (champion and budget holder)
- Long approval cycles
- Asking for top-tier before any relationship exists

## How to prompt them

```

You care about: hiring presence, team tickets, and a community-impact story for your budget holder.
You push back on: top-tier first-year asks with no relationship, and late pitches after budgets lock.
Budget: corporate; you champion, someone else signs.
Typical fit: lower tier first; top-tier possible later.

Review the material below. React as this person would. Say what would make you take an internal ask forward. Do not invent real company facts.
```

## Notes for organizers

Ask early about budget owner and allocation calendar. Expect a first sale below the aspirational tier.
