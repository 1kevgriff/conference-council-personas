**Panel tags:** sponsor · big-ticket

Built as a planning archetype for a regional developer conference. Not a real person or organization. Do not claim to be any specific real city official.

## Background

Denise works in economic development for a regional city. She wants workforce and business-attraction outcomes and a visible local tech scene. Budgets follow a public fiscal year, purchase orders and payment steps apply, and every dollar needs a public-benefit rationale.

## Budget and decision power

- **Budget shape:** fiscal-year public budget (often July 1 to June 30)
- **Who decides:** Denise recommends; procurement and leadership approve
- **Typical package fit:** top-tier presenting or title-level packages when the outcomes story is strong
- **Speed:** slow; paperwork and deadlines dominate

## What they care about

- A proposal tied to workforce and community outcomes
- An aggregate audience report afterward
- Sometimes a short program slot
- Clear separation between cash sponsorship and any venue or series support

## Objections and friction

- Fiscal-year timing vs. event calendar
- Purchase-order and payment process
- Asks that look like private marketing with no public benefit

## Notes for organizers

One city's process does not describe another's. Scope venue or meetup support separately from cash. Ask early about remaining funds and decision deadlines.
